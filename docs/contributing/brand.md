# Logos and colors

![MeridianDB symbol](../assets/brand/meridiandb-icon-180.png)

Two adjoining arches form MeridianDB's compact **m** symbol. The paired curves
suggest meridians and the database's two dimensions of time. A navy background
keeps the white and mint strokes readable on light and dark surfaces.

## Download the artwork

SVG files contain vector geometry, including outlined wordmark lettering. They
render without an installed font or external resource. PNG exports have transparent
backgrounds; the square icon includes its navy tile with transparent outer corners.

| Use | SVG | PNG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub avatar or large square icon | [Square symbol](../assets/brand/meridiandb-icon.svg) | [1024 × 1024](../assets/brand/meridiandb-github-1024.png), [512 × 512](../assets/brand/meridiandb-icon-512.png) |
| Website header or app icon | [Square symbol](../assets/brand/meridiandb-icon.svg) | [180 × 180](../assets/brand/meridiandb-icon-180.png) |
| Favicon | [Square symbol](../assets/brand/meridiandb-icon.svg) | [32 × 32](../assets/brand/meridiandb-icon-32.png) |
| Wordmark on light backgrounds | [Navy and teal](../assets/brand/meridiandb-wordmark.svg) | [2148 × 512](../assets/brand/meridiandb-wordmark.png) |
| Wordmark on dark backgrounds | [White and mint](../assets/brand/meridiandb-wordmark-dark.svg) | [2148 × 512](../assets/brand/meridiandb-wordmark-dark.png) |

Preserve the aspect ratio and clear space around the artwork. Use the square
symbol for small placements; use the wordmark where the full name remains legible.
The avatar's main strokes fit within a circular crop. Choose the wordmark variant
that contrasts with its background.

## Palette and type

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep navy | `#102C38` | Icon background and light-surface lettering |
| Mint | `#5EEAD4` | Second arch and dark-surface DB lettering |
| Soft white | `#F0FDFA` | First arch and dark-surface lettering |
| Teal | `#0F766E` | DB lettering on light surfaces |

The original symbol follows the repository's [Apache-2.0 license](https://github.com/MeridianDB/meridiandb/blob/main/LICENSE).
The wordmark uses outlined **Inter 4.1 SemiBold**, from
[the Inter project](https://github.com/rsms/inter/releases/tag/v4.1). The font's
[SIL Open Font License and attribution](../assets/brand/INTER-LICENSE.txt) accompany
the artwork. Font files are not required by the website.
